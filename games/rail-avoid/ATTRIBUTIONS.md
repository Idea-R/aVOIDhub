# Attributions

## Original content

All art, audio and content in RAILaVOID (map tiles, train and enemy sprites, effects, the procedural music and sound effects, the world generator, the passenger events and all text) are generated procedurally in code by aVOID Games for this project. No third-party images, audio samples or fonts are bundled.

## Fonts

System fonts only: `'Segoe UI', system-ui, -apple-system, Roboto, sans-serif` and the browser's monospace stack. No web fonts are downloaded or embedded.

## Music

The soundtrack in `public/audio/*.mp3` (title, calm, tense, combat, boss, victory, defeat) was generated by the project owner with **Suno v5.5** (https://suno.com) from prompts written for this game (instrumental only, no vocals), under the owner's Suno subscription terms. Prompts are recorded in `public/audio/manifest.json`. If the files are absent the game falls back to the built-in procedural score (`src/audio/music.ts`).
